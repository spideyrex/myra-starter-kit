### Surface appearance — settings, engine and degradation (bundle A)

**Added**

- `App\Appearance` domain: `Background`, `BackgroundType`, `Scrim`, `Recipes`,
  `CssValue`, `AuthLayout`, `AuthLayoutRegistry`, `AuthAppearance`,
  `AppearanceManager` and the `Appearance` facade.
- Four auth layout declarations (`split`, `centered`, `cover`, `card`) wired
  through `config('myra.appearance.auth_layouts')`.
- Fourteen additive `appearance` settings rows (migration
  `2026_08_29_000001`), mirrored as typed properties on `AppearanceSettings`.
- `resources/js/Layouts/Guest/SplitLayout.vue` — today's login shell, extracted
  verbatim — and `resources/js/composables/useAppearance.ts`.
- `php artisan myra:appearance reset [--surface=auth|page|all]`, the break-glass
  path that works when you are already locked out.
- `MYRA_AUTH_APPEARANCE=false` — a kill switch that returns the stock split
  layout with no settings read at all.

**Changed**

- `GuestLayout.vue` is now a dispatcher over statically bundled shells
  (`import.meta.glob(..., { eager: true })`; no `defineAsyncComponent` anywhere
  on the login route).
- `BrandManager::styleTag()` delegates its character allowlist to
  `CssValue::safe()` — one sanitiser instead of two. No behaviour change.
- New unconditional Inertia prop `appearance` and a `<meta name="myra-appearance">`
  tag; `<style id="myra-surface">` is emitted after the brand tokens.

**Guarantees**

- Upgrading changes nothing: at the shipped defaults both surfaces return `[]`
  from `cssVars()` and `styleTag()` renders the empty string.
- Contrast is server-computed (`BrandPalette::foregroundOn`, a measured WCAG
  black/white pick), floored by a mandatory scrim on image-backed surfaces and
  backstopped by card isolation of the form. Saving is never blocked.
- Six independent layers of degradation, each of which alone yields a working
  login page.
